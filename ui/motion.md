# Motion

- Motion should communicate state, relationships, or feedback. It should not obscure actions or delay completion.
- Follow the platform's reduced-motion preferences where supported. Provide a suitable nonanimated alternative to essential motion.
- Avoid continuous decorative animation, disruptive entrance sequences, and unnecessary layout movement.
- Ensure transitions do not prevent interaction or interfere with accessibility and automated tests.
- Do not prescribe specific animation libraries or durations across platforms.
